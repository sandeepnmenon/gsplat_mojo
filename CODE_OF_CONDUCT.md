# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project. Be constructive,
assume neither expertise nor intent, critique ideas rather than people, respect
privacy, and make space for different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, sustained disruption, deliberate intimidation, publication of
private information, or retaliation against a good-faith report.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, reviews, project events,
and other spaces where someone represents this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not open a public issue about conduct involving private or sensitive
information. The project does not yet publish a confidential conduct-reporting
address. Until one is configured, ask a repository maintainer through their
GitHub profile for a private reporting route without including report details.
This missing durable intake route is a governance blocker for release.

Maintainers may remove content, issue warnings, limit participation, or ban
participants according to severity and context. Reports will be handled as
privately and promptly as maintainer capacity allows; no response-time
guarantee is currently offered.

This policy is project-authored and may be revised as governance matures.
